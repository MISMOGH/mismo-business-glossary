Variations on C — routine visible, the rest behind labelled drawers

Same structure throughout: the glossary, what needs a decision, what is unpublished, and whether the work is safe — with anything that replaces work kept behind a heading that says so. What changes is the shape: how the numbers are presented, how much shows at rest, and how loudly the panel signals that something needs doing.

C1

Verdict banner, numbers in a grid

A coloured band at the top answers "is anything wrong?" before you read anything. The four counts become a grid rather than a list, which halves the height they take.

Trade-off: every fact visible with no scrolling and no clicking, which suits a facilitator who checks the same four numbers each time. The least forgiving if the panel ever grows.

Choosing between them

C1 and C2 put effort into signalling state; C3 and C4 put it into saving space. If the facilitator works in long sessions with the term list open, C3 gives the most room. If they dip in to check whether anything needs doing, C1 answers that before they read.

All four keep the same two drawers, and all four show the backup with a time. They can also be mixed — C1's banner sits fine on top of C4's dense body, for instance.
