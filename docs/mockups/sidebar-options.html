Console sidebar — three reimagined versions

All three add the time to the last backup, and fold Repository and More together. Both of those headings name the machinery rather than the job: a facilitator has no reason to know what a repository is, and "More" says nothing at all.

They also separate two things the current sidebar runs together. The draft save is automatic and is the real safety net. The backup is a file you download by hand and is an escape hatch. Sitting adjacent under different headings, they read as two versions of the same thing.

Option A

Say it in sentences

Leads with a plain statement of whether the work is safe, with all three timestamps in one block so they can be compared at a glance. Everything else is grouped by what it is for, not by where it goes.

Saving & history

Your draft saves itself online every hour. A backup is a copy on your own computer.

Why: "Everything is saved" is the question being asked; the timestamps answer it. Putting all three together also makes a stale backup obvious, which two separate sections hid.

Option B

A timeline of the work

Treats saving as a sequence rather than a set of buttons: what is published, what is saved online, what is on your machine. The order is the order things happen in, so where the work currently stands is readable without knowing any of the terminology.

Backups & earlier versions

A backup is a single file on your computer holding the draft, the classifications and the release history.

Replace what is in this browser

These discard what is here and load from elsewhere. Save the draft first if it is ahead.

Why: import, restore and load-from-online are rare and each replaces work. Grouping them under a heading that says so is a guardrail as much as a tidy-up — they sat one click from "Download a backup" in a list called "More".

What they share

All three drop the words Repository and More. They give the backup a time as well as a date, and they say in plain words that the draft saves itself — which is the single most reassuring fact in the panel and was previously only implied by a timestamp.

They differ in how much is visible at once. A shows everything and leads with a verdict. B shows everything and orders it by what happens when. C shows the routine four and folds the rest away behind headings that warn what is inside.
