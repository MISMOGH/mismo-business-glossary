The two headline numbers lead, the classification counts and their button sit directly beneath, and C2's coloured sections carry on below. The three versions differ only in how the header and the "needs a decision" section divide the work between them — the duplication is the one thing the merge has to resolve.

Trade-off: the amber bar appears only when it is earned, so a settled panel is calm and a busy one is obvious. Costs a little height when something is outstanding, which is precisely when height is worth spending.

The thing to decide

Putting the issue count in the header means the old "Needs a decision" section has little left to do. Merge 1 keeps both and accepts the repetition. Merge 2 drops the section and makes the number itself the control, which is tidiest but loses the one-line explanation. Merge 3 keeps the explanation and the button but attaches them to the header, and shows nothing at all when there is nothing outstanding — so the panel is quiet on a normal day and loud on the day it matters.
